Why I built it

Every other launcher either died, got slow, or showed me a browser in an app. I just wanted a list of my worlds and a Play button that worked.

Modrinth inside

Search, install, and manage Modrinth packs directly from the launcher.

Each world is its own box

Version pinning, custom Java, and separate installs for each instance.

Choose your loader

Fabric, Forge, Quilt, and NeoForge support.

Small Electron shell on purpose. Most of your RAM should go to Minecraft, not to me. Sign-in is Microsoft OAuth, and Java is managed per instance.
