note from me

Why I built it

I built Crispy because downloading and playing Minecraft should be completely simple — 100% free with zero paywalls. Plus, it's community-driven: if there's a feature you want added to the launcher, just jump in and ask!

🧀

Modrinth inside

Install mods without leaving the launcher. Search, sort, add, and keep your modpacks tidy.

Each world is its own box

Version pinning, custom Java, separate installs, no more reinstalling everything just to test one mod.

Fabric / Forge / Quilt / NeoForge

Pick your loader and stay focused on the build that actually runs on your machine.

Small Electron shell on purpose. Most of your RAM should go to Minecraft, not to me. Sign-in is Microsoft OAuth — tokens stay local to your account, and Java is managed per instance.
