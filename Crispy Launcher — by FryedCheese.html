note from me

Why I built it

Every other launcher either died, got slow, or showed me a browser in an app. I just wanted a list of my worlds and a Play button that worked first try. So I made Crispy — small, brown, a bit scrappy, but it launches.

🧀

Modrinth inside

Search in-app, install mods/shaders/packs. Deps come too. No alt-tabbing to a website.

Each world in its own folder

Break a mod? Your other worlds are fine. Copy the folder to back up.

Fabric / Forge / Quilt / NeoForge

Pick one when you make the instance. It grabs the right Java too.

Small Electron shell on purpose. Most of your RAM should go to Minecraft, not to me. Sign-in is Microsoft OAuth — tokens stay on your PC.
